# ADR-0012: Course title

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-03

## Context

The title must work in English and Spanish, speak directly to the audience, and signal
that the course fills a gap in their knowledge. "The Missing Manual" was the original
inspiration but is a trademarked book series.

## Decision

- English: **Automation & AI for Business Users: What They Never Taught You**
- Spanish: **Automatización e IA para profesionales: lo que nunca te enseñaron**
- Short form for the site header and browser tab: "Automation & AI for Business Users" / "Automatización e IA para profesionales".

## Consequences

The long title appears on the home page hero, README, and social cards; the short form
is the Starlight `title`. Spanish targets "profesionales" rather than a literal
translation of "business users".
