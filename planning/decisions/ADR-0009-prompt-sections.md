# ADR-0009: Every lesson includes a good vs lousy prompt comparison

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-03

## Context

Learners will use AI assistants constantly. Prompting taught in a single chapter is forgotten; taught in context on every page it becomes a habit.

## Decision

Every lesson includes an 'Ask your AI assistant' section, implemented with the PromptExample component: lousy prompt, what goes wrong, good prompt, why it works, what to check. Lesson 2.7 teaches the prompt anatomy that all sections reference.

## Consequences

More authoring effort per lesson. The lousy prompts must be realistic, not strawmen. Prompts are translated in the Spanish version.
