## Why this matters

Business is organised by time: days, weeks, months, quarters. Once dates are real
dates, not text, you can group, compare, and sort by them. Time zones add a second layer: the same moment has
different clock times in San José and in a cloud server in another country.

<CaseStudy>
	The owner wants weekly sales and to know the busiest weekday. Later, a cloud
	service logs orders in UTC, and Daniela must match them with local sales.
</CaseStudy>

## Dates as dates

Lesson 6.4 converted the mixed text into real dates. A real date column has the type
`datetime64`, and gives access to parts through `.dt`: `.dt.day_name()`, `.dt.month`,
`.dt.isocalendar().week`.

Always check the order of day and month when converting (lesson 4.12). The sales file
proves it: `13/01/2024` can only be day first.

## Grouping by time

`resample` groups by a time period, like a group-by for dates. It needs the date as the
index.

<Example file="part06/08_dates.py" />

- `resample("W-SUN")` makes weeks that end on Sunday. Other codes: `"D"` days, `"MS"` months.
- The last week ends on 4 February because the file's last days (29 to 31 January) fall in the week that finishes then.
- `.dt.day_name()` gives the weekday, and `value_counts()` shows the busiest: Wednesday.

## Time zones

A date and time without a zone is **naive**. It is ambiguous. With a zone it is **aware**.
Costa Rica has no daylight saving time, and is at UTC-6 all year, but many places change
their clocks twice a year, which makes calculations tricky.

<Mermaid
	diagram={`flowchart LR
  A["08:00 San José, naive"] -->|"tz_localize America/Costa_Rica"| B["08:00 -06:00, aware"]
  B -->|"tz_convert UTC"| C["14:00 UTC"]`}
/>

`tz_localize` attaches a zone to a naive time. `tz_convert` changes an aware time to another
zone. The example shows 08:00 in Costa Rica as 14:00 UTC.

Rules: store times in **UTC**, convert to local time only for display, and never compare
a naive time with an aware one.

## Common mistakes

- **Keeping dates as text.** Sorting and grouping then use text order.
- **Swapping day and month.**
- **Mixing naive and aware times.**
- **Ignoring daylight saving elsewhere.** Use named zones, not fixed offsets.
- **Grouping by week without saying where the week starts.**
