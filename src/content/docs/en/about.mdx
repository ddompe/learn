## Hi, I'm Diego

I'm a Distinguished Technologist at an international technology company, based in
Costa Rica. Over more than 20 years I've worked across embedded systems, Linux,
networking hardware, compilers, infrastructure, and open source. I hold several
patents, and I've spent much of that time building teams and helping engineers grow.

## Why I made this

AI has put automation within reach of everyone, but many capable professionals get
stuck on fundamentals that nobody explained to them. This course fills that gap with
one opinionated, practical stack, chosen to anticipate the engineering problems you
would otherwise discover the hard way.
